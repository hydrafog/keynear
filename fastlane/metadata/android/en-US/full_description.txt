Keynear pairs a Linux desktop with an Android phone over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and local Wi-Fi QUIC with zero cloud accounts and zero relay servers.

Key capabilities:
- Resumable 60 KiB checkpointed file and directory streaming across LAN and Wi-Fi fallback transitions
- Privacy-aware bidirectional clipboard synchronization with password-manager MIME exclusion
- PipeWire and MPRIS2 Bluetooth headset handoff
- Native Android DocumentsProvider surfacing paired Linux shared directories in the system Files app
- Noise XX mutual authentication and post-quantum hybrid X25519MLKEM768 transport encryption

The F-Droid build uses the foss flavor with zero Google Play Services dependencies.
